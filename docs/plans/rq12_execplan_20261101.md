# RQ2 compound-fault mechanisms and RQ1 independent confirmation

## Purpose
Deepen channel-level trust research by identifying when two simultaneous faulty sensors corrupt a shared regime reference, then test at most one lightweight remedy. Confirm coverage and conditioning effects independently without changing the frozen RQ3 or English manuscript. Overall deadline remains November 28.

## Scientific assumptions
All new observations/interventions are synthetic. RQ2 uses old validation batches, not fresh confirmation; paired perturbations do not multiply independent sample size. Two-channel faults use per-regime scales from original train references; signs and offsets are balanced independently of observations. True regimes and fault masks are evaluation-only. No real process reconstruction or quality prediction.
